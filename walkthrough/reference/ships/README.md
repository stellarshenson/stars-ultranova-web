# Ship Reference Board

Inspiration imagery for the ships3d rebuild - studied for proportion and anatomy, never shipped. Everything in this folder is copyrighted concept or game art (Games Workshop, Volition, Alcon/Amazon) used strictly as visual reference; no pixel and no geometry from here enters `assets/`. Clean-licensed donor geometry lives in `assets/ships/donors/` and is ledgered in `assets/ships/LICENSES.md`.

## Anatomy digests - the six warship classes

Proportions were measured off the board images (pixel ratios on the profile and ortho views), then rounded; they are targets for the compiler records, not decoration. Common law from the board: the W40k capitals are LONG and DEEP, never wide - beam is the smallest dimension, and dorsal-ventral height (spine towers to keel structures) exceeds beam on everything cruiser-weight and up. The prow is closed armoured mass. The stern is a housed cluster of engine bells. FreeSpace supplies the surface language at all sizes: chamfered facets, greeble trenches, turret blisters, glowing apertures.

**Frigate** - smallest line warship, Expanse logic in a FreeSpace skin: the Rocinante's thrust-axis stack tipped horizontal, everything visibly functional. Length to beam about 4.5:1, section near-square (height roughly equal to beam) with heavy corner chamfers, Orion-style. Prow is a solid plough wedge taking the front quarter, deep V in both profile and plan - no framework, no mandibles at this weight. No broadside: armament is two to three turret blisters on dorsal and ventral centreline plus RCS pods at the shoulder line. Bridge is a low dorsal notch at the aft third, not a tower. Stern block carries two thruster bells recessed in a shared armoured housing with radiator fins as its edges.

**Destroyer** - the proven proof-hull anatomy, one step up. Length to beam about 5.5:1, hull depth about 1.2x beam so the profile already reads deeper than wide. Galley-ram prow at about 22% of length: layered plough beak wider than deep, ram beam reinforced like stacked leaf armour. A single spinal weapons trench runs the dorsal midline (Orion trench language), with two to three turret blisters per flank low on the chine - not yet a gun deck. Modest aft superstructure block, one deck tall, crowned by sensor masts rather than towers. Three bells in the stern housing, glow reading at sheet scale.

**Cruiser** - the Lunar-class is the anchor and the first true broadside hull. Length to beam about 6.5:1, depth about 1.4x beam. Ram prow takes the front quarter: armoured plough plate over a projecting ram spike, exactly the Armada 2 read. One casemate row per flank - repeated gun-port bays with barrels, spanning the middle 40% of length - becomes the visual centre of the flank. Dorsal spine grows its first crenellation: a run of short towers and vents aft of midships. Bridge is a stern-castle block, distinctly taller than the hull, set at the aft sixth. Three bells clustered in an armoured engine block, ventral keel fin under the stern quarter.

**Battle Cruiser** - a stretched cruiser that out-runs its armour. Longest ratio in the line: length to beam about 7.5:1, depth about 1.5x beam, the leanest and fastest-looking silhouette. Ram is longer and sharper than the cruiser's, about 28% of length, gothic beak permitted. Single broadside casemate row per flank plus a dorsal rank of trainable turret blisters - more guns than the cruiser but one tier, never two. Spine towers taller and thinner than the cruiser's; bridge castle raked. Four bells in a wider, flatter stern housing with prominent radiator wings - the engine block is oversized for the hull and should look it.

**Battleship** - the Retribution-class render is the definition. Length to beam about 7:1, hull depth about 1.6x beam and about 2.5x with the spine towers counted. Solid wedge prow at the front fifth, the white armoured plough carrying an emblem. The flank IS the ship: two full gun-deck tiers of casemate batteries running about 55% of the length amidships, individually barrelled, lit from within. Crenellated dorsal spine of cathedral towers over the battery span; dense emissive window fields reading as a crewed city. Massive stern-castle bridge with flanking wings, the tallest point of the ship. Three giant bells in an armoured engine block with integral radiator fins.

**Dreadnought** - Oberon mass at Despoiler menace: the cathedral on its side. Length to beam about 6.5:1 but depth grows to about 2x beam - it out-deeps the battleship rather than out-lengthening it. Ram beak is comparatively short, about 18% of length: mass over reach, wider than the battleship's and blunter. Three tiered broadside decks per flank, the tiers stepped like the Oberon profile, plus ventral gun platforms hung beneath the keel - guns above, below and beside. The spine is a continuous fortified city from midships to stern, no gap between towers. Bridge castle at the stern is the highest structure, buttressed forward. Four to five bells in a tiered engine block, exhausts stacked in two ranks, radiators as flank walls of the stern quarter.

## Donor coverage - honest inventory (32 hull records)

No clean-licensed donor found this round approaches the required intricacy as a whole-hull base - everything CC0/CC-BY out there is low-poly stylised, and the art bar (intricate, realistic, W40k-like) rules them out as bases. Verdict per class is therefore parts-only or fully procedural; the compiler remains the hull author for all 32, driven by the digests above.

- **Kitbash parts exist** (donor parts from `assets/ships/donors/` genuinely usable as instanced detail):
  - Frigate, Destroyer, Cruiser, Battle Cruiser, Battleship, Dreadnought - Kenney turrets as turret-blister seeds, rocket bases as engine bells, satellite dishes as sensor gardens; Majadroid weapon components
  - Small/Medium/Large/Super Freighter, Galleon - Kenney cargo pods, barrels, pipework, machine housings; Majadroid hull segments
  - Fuel Transport, Super-Fuel Transport - Kenney rocket fuel stages and barrel clusters as external tank stacks
  - Midget/Mini/Miner/Maxi/Ultra Miner - Kenney `craft_miner`, machine housings, pipework, supports
  - Mini Mine Layer, Super Mine Layer - Kenney barrel racks (`barrels_rail`) as mine dispensers
  - Mini Bomber, B-17 Bomber, B-52 Bomber - Kenney rocket stages as hung ordnance on the rack spine
  - Assault Transport - Kenney cargo pods as drop-craft, Majadroid accessories
  - Colony Ship, Mini-Colony Ship - Kenney machine housings and structure trusses for the hab modules
- **Fully procedural** (no donor content helps; digests and board only): Scout, Privateer, Rogue, Stealth Bomber, Nubian, Meta Morph
- **Donor base exists**: none. The 11 Quaternius whole ships are kept as silhouette and blockout references at the toy end of the spectrum, not as shipping bases
