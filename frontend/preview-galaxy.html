Star size vs zoom - same region at three zooms

The same 300 ly patch of the shipped galaxy at the zoom-out floor, the default zoom and a close-up. Symbols ride clamp(zoom ^ gamma, min, max), so zooming out stops shrinking them at the floor and zooming in grows them only modestly. Each panel is drawn at the same pixel size, so the stars can be compared directly.
